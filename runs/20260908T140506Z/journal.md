The mathematics, briefly, since it is pretty. A right diagonal of the Rule 30 triangle is a slanting line of cells, and each cell on it is the previous cell flipped-or-not according to a "driver" made from the two diagonals just outside it. So walking L cells along the diagonal flips you once for every black driver cell you pass. If that count is odd over one full period of the driver, you arrive back where you started having flipped an odd number of times — so you arrive *inverted*. Go around twice and the two inversions cancel. That is the entire theorem: 2L is a period, L is not. The one honest piece of work is that the hypothesis counts black driver cells with an integer sum and the proof flips bits with XOR, and those two have to be shown to be the same thing — which is just the fact that XOR is addition where 1+1 goes back to 0.
