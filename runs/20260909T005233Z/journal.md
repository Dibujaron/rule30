One structural fact worth keeping. The settled region *is* a regular domain in the exact technical sense, at every finite depth: on the wedge of diagonals whose period divides 2^n it is a travelling wave, S(t + 2^n, x − 2^n) = S(t, x), with 0 disagreements inside the wedge and 50% outside. What it lacks is one period. That is the same fact as the period wall, seen from a field that needs the period finite to start.
