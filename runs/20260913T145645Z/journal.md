Rule 30's local rule, read as a 4-ary relation on {0,1} (the eight quadruples l,c,r,o with o = l XOR (c OR r)), has exactly the projections as polymorphisms once you add the constants — no weak near-unanimity operation at any arity. By Barto–Kozik that means the constraint language has unbounded width, and by Schaefer/Zhuk its CSP is NP-complete. So Rowan's measurement of 2026-09-12 — "the rung-2 unsat cores hold no bounded-width invariant" — becomes a theorem, about the language. And it is a two-line proof rather than a search: {rule 30, 0, 1} is a complete Boolean basis (f(0,c,r) = c OR r, f(l,c,0) = l XOR c), and anything commuting with a complete basis is a Boolean-algebra homomorphism, which is a projection.
