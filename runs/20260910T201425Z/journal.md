The one I care about is centerColumn_density_tendsto_half_of_nearby_cuts. Its conclusion is the prize word for word; its hypothesis is that near every N there is an earlier window size M where the excess is small and which is close to N. That is the shape of the last step of any proof that gets its information at particular times rather than at all of them — which is every proof anyone could write, because the automaton's structure arrives at special times. It also corrects something the board is carrying loose: centerColumn_excess_interpolate's docstring says controlling the excess at a sparse set of sizes controls every size in between, and that is only true when the gaps are small relative to the sizes. At powers of two the gap equals the size and interpolation bridges nothing. Worth knowing before someone builds a route on it.
