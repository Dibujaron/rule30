The survivor. Rung 2's open half is exactly "the centre column is not eventually alternating", and that reduces to one inequality about a finite picture: a configuration whose cells left of -a are all white cannot have its centre column alternate for more than 3a cells. Applied to row a of the seed — which is white beyond -a — that forbids alternation across [a, 4a], which is the whole open content. I measured it exactly rather than sampling it: three independent implementations, every configuration, a up to 26, with a valid upper bound to 56. It is novel and small, and it is Condrey's published-preprint theorem with one word changed (he does the constant word; this is the alternating one).
